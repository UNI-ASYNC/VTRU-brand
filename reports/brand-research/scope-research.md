# Scope public-site research

Checked **13 November 2026**. Read-only current app inspection; no wallet or transaction actions.

Scope is the Foundation’s first-party Vitruveo asset-management application, not simply an analytics/explorer brand. Public routes already include tokenomics, multisig, staking, VTRU/USDT bridges, VNS, VIBE, Reflect and contract information. The existing About/release-history page is at https://scope.vitruveo.ai/info/about and shows v0.8.9. It was not linked in the inspected Info menu.

The app has a favicon: https://scope.vitruveo.ai/favicon.ico. It is byte-identical to Vitruveo’s white symbol favicon, with16/32/48px images. Client rendering adds the title/description VTRU Scope by Vitruveo; no OG/Twitter metadata or manifest/icon link declarations were observed in the rendered head. The conventional icon still works as a browser fallback.

Reuse the product routes and existing About/release notes. Improvements should focus on discoverability, share previews and confirmation of the intended short/long brand name.
