# Vertical Foundation: website and relationship audit

Checked **2026-11-13**. Read-only research; no assets, accounts, uploads, transactions, or website changes were made. Public statements below are evidence of the site's own positioning, rather than independent verification of legal status, launch performance, financial arrangements, or ownership.

## Current destination and domain evidence

Use **[www.verticalfoundation.net](https://www.verticalfoundation.net/)** as the working Foundation destination. The homepage, [news](https://www.verticalfoundation.net/news/), and [validator page](https://www.verticalfoundation.net/validators/) returned HTTP 200. The `.net` apex redirected to `www` with HTTP 307. There was no `rel=canonical` link in the homepage HTML examined.

The old-domain HTTP URL, [http://www.verticalfnd.com/](http://www.verticalfnd.com/), redirected with HTTP 302 directly to `https://www.verticalfoundation.net/`. The corresponding HTTPS `www.verticalfnd.com` requests returned 503 in this session; apex HTTPS returned a tunnel/proxy 403. This does not establish a universal outage. [Vitruveo's current homepage](https://www.vitruveo.ai/) still links the HTTPS `.com` address, while [Pretrend's current homepage](https://www.pretrend.ai/) links `.net`. Update the lingering link when the owner confirms the preferred domain.

The search reader exposed a four-month-old cached `.com` narrative featuring “Verticals” and Vibe Legal. It is useful historical context, but its legal-product narrative is not a basis for current Foundation promotional copy. The redirect and newer `.net` pages are stronger evidence of present positioning.

## Relationship architecture supported by the current pages

The [Foundation homepage](https://www.verticalfoundation.net/) positions the Foundation as Vitruveo's governing and funding body. Its six pillars are **VTRU, Pretrend, VIBE, Scope, Protocol, and Validators**. Treat them as different kinds of ecosystem components, rather than six interchangeable company brands:

| Component | Published role | Implication for the brand system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vertical Foundation | Governance, treasury stewardship, development and network coordination | Institutional parent/steward identity |
| Vitruveo | EVM-compatible Layer 1 network | Network identity; VTRU is its native asset |
| Pretrend | Prediction-market trend protocol/product | Distinct project identity with Foundation attribution |
| Scope | First-party asset-management application | Product identity; source kit calls it VTRU Scope, public copy shortens it to Scope |
| VIBE | Vitruveo Income Building Engine | Community income/revenue-sharing program; distinct from historical Vibe Legal |
| Protocol / Validators | Chain capabilities and network operation | Service/program communication layers, not automatically new logo families |

[Pretrend's current footer](https://www.pretrend.ai/) identifies it as a Vertical Foundation project and links the Foundation. Its current homepage also links AI Wars Trends on `epicaiwars.com`. AI Wars is not one of the Foundation's six listed pillars; that absence does not prove discontinuation or disaffiliation.

## Current announcements and editorial tensions

The fresh HTTP HTML for [news](https://www.verticalfoundation.net/news/) includes **“Pretrend Is Live,” November 11, 2026**, above the July 1 Foundation establishment announcement. It describes the TrendPulse feed, Pump.fun five-minute market, VUSD and swap as live, with betting opening within 24 hours of publication. Treat the timing as dated announcement copy; do not reuse the relative deadline as a current call to action.

The July 1 article states that the Foundation intended to incorporate, identifies founding handles phantom, ctitch and techbubble, and describes a planned Yieldable acquisition. Neither the establishment article nor the pages examined proves incorporation or acquisition completion. The same historical article says other projects were deprecated to focus on Pretrend; current Pretrend links to AI Wars, so that historical sentence should not be turned into a blanket present-day status label.

The homepage describes cross-chain Pretrend resolution, whereas the November announcement describes a Vitruveo/VUSD reference market. These may describe different layers or phases; reconcile deployment-specific copy with the current Pretrend documentation before putting architecture/version claims in permanent collateral.

The cached [old `/pretrend/` page](https://www.verticalfoundation.net/pretrend/) describes Version 2.0 and a 2026 roadmap. Fresh requests returned **404**. Do not use its cached roadmap as current launch status.

## Existing copy and resources to reuse

The current homepage's short line, **“The foundation governing and funding the Vitruveo ecosystem.”**, is a defensible existing-copy starting point. Keep six-pillar names, Foundation project attribution and neutral stewardship language. Website publication does not substitute for the owner's approval of new official collateral.

The public contact is **[info@verticalfoundation.net](mailto:info@verticalfoundation.net)**, linked from homepage, news and validator-page footers. No physical address or incorporated legal-entity name was found in those pages. Use editable fields for those details.

Existing resources include the [news archive](https://www.verticalfoundation.net/news/), [validator onboarding and calculator page](https://www.verticalfoundation.net/validators/), [Scope Legacy link](https://scope.vitruveo.ai/legacy), [Vitruveo developer hub](https://www.vitruveo.ai/developers), and the Foundation article's [HOST paper destination](https://kalyani.com/). The latter is an HTML technical paper, not a confirmed PDF download. Its [trend-paper navigation](https://kalyani.com/trend.html) was discovered but not read. The article's “Pretrend whitepaper” link currently points to the [Pretrend homepage](https://www.pretrend.ai/), not directly to a paper.

The Foundation homepage exposes its existing logo, favicon, application icons and social-card metadata. Those are website resources; the user's uploaded ZIP remains the stated artwork source of truth. Root audit covers the image files and their dimensions.

## Reuse / improve / new / needs confirmation

- **Reuse:** the supplied protected marks; current Foundation role; six-pillar terminology; public contact; existing news/developer/validator resources.
- **Improve:** consistent domain links; date-aware announcement templates; distinguish Foundation stewardship, network, token, applications and programs; pair “VTRU Scope” source artwork with confirmed editorial naming.
- **New:** a current relationship diagram, media index and short approved-copy library can be authored from this evidence. No such new collateral was created during this research task.
- **Needs confirmation:** preferred Foundation domain and repair of the HTTPS legacy route; Scope's long/short editorial name; incorporation/acquisition completion if those facts are needed; current VIBE destination; how historical deprecation language relates to current AI Wars links; deployment/version language for Pretrend.

## Not found in the audited scope

No separate brand/press-download page, brand-guidelines PDF, presentation/deck, direct social profile, Discord invite, privacy/terms page, charter/bylaws, public governance-voting page or registration document was linked from the three current Foundation pages read. Targeted site searches produced no additional resource results. This is a bounded finding, not a claim that no such resource exists elsewhere. `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` returned 404.

The homepage's VIBE CTA points to [scope.vitruveo.ai/vibe](https://scope.vitruveo.ai/vibe); the search fetch returned 404. The current validator page provides a support offer alongside paid node plans; its calculator explicitly labels prices hypothetical and results as projections. Those numbers are unsuitable as timeless brand messaging without a dated product owner review.
