# Pretrend — existing-site research

Checked **13 November 2026**. Read-only audit; current HTTPS responses take precedence over search-cache extracts. No sign-in, wallet, swap or market action was performed.

## What already exists

Pretrend has a working public information/product structure: a [market discovery homepage](https://www.pretrend.ai/), [protocol page](https://www.pretrend.ai/protocol/), [whitepaper](https://www.pretrend.ai/whitepaper/), [OLS explainer](https://www.pretrend.ai/ols/), [historical replays and precompile examples](https://www.pretrend.ai/examples/), [TrendPulse status](https://www.pretrend.ai/trendpulse/), [wallet-based leaderboard](https://www.pretrend.ai/leaderboard/), [VUSD funding UI](https://www.pretrend.ai/swap/), and market pages. These resources should be reused and linked before assuming they need creating.

The homepage offers [pump.fun5-minute](https://www.pretrend.ai/markets/pumpfun-5/), [Bitcoin15-minute](https://www.pretrend.ai/markets/bitcoin-15/) and [Ethereum30-minute](https://www.pretrend.ai/markets/ethereum-30/) routes, plus [AIWars Trends](https://www.epicaiwars.com/trends). ETH is explicitly read-only. The current [TrendPulse](https://www.pretrend.ai/trendpulse/) copy labels AIWars telemetry simulated, rather than the finished game's live feed.
