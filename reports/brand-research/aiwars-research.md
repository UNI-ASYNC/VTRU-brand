# AI Wars public-site research

Checked **13 November 2026**. This is a read-only review of current public pages, with no wallet, mint, bet, purchase or signed-message interaction.

AI Wars already has a substantial public site. Its current positioning is a persistent science-fiction game universe, with a separate Trends engagement. The homepage identifies it as a Vertical Foundation project and links the ecosystem brands. A replacement generic landing page would duplicate existing material. [Homepage](https://www.epicaiwars.com/)

The whitepaper puts gameplay first and describes Vitruveo as durable history/ownership infrastructure beneath real-time play. The audience implied by that material is science-fiction/action-game players; no audience size was verified. [Whitepaper](https://www.epicaiwars.com/whitepaper)

The immediate copy issue is status reconciliation: the roadmap calls Genesis and shard minting production features, while their entry pages display opening soon. That mismatch needs owner confirmation before announcing launch. [Roadmap](https://www.epicaiwars.com/roadmap), [Genesis](https://www.epicaiwars.com/genesis), [Mint](https://www.epicaiwars.com/mint)

Treat the live-labelled Trends interface, the simulated current feed and the planned main game as three different status statements. Do not reuse an older Trends-only description as the full AI Wars proposition. [Trends](https://www.epicaiwars.com/trends)

Existing acquisition paths address the Vitruveo community, prospective shard holders and wallet-enabled Trends participants. The latter has stake/reward conditions rather than a general free-play promise. These are inferred audience segments from the public journeys, not verified demographics. [Genesis](https://www.epicaiwars.com/genesis), [Shard page](https://www.epicaiwars.com/nft), [Trends](https://www.epicaiwars.com/trends)

Reuse existing approved lore imagery, canonical documents and product explanations. Campaign materials can improve concise orientation and link people to those resources. Root is separately auditing actual image/icon/social-preview files; this review does not certify licensing or final artwork approval. [Homepage](https://www.epicaiwars.com/)

A discoverable support/contact entry, press/brand resource index and social directory were not observed in the reviewed navigation. That is a possible discoverability gap, not a claim those resources do not exist elsewhere or privately. Check with the owner before creating replacements.
