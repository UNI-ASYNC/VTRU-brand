# Ecosystem website and brand resource research

Research checked **13 November 2026**. This date records the research, rather than a deadline for implementation. The review covered the five public websites, linked documentation and resources, live image/icon files, and the rendered Scope application. No wallet was connected and no purchase, mint, market or other transaction was performed.

## Main finding

The ecosystem already has substantial public information, product journeys and brand assets. The useful next work is to make those resources easier to find, bring current descriptions into agreement, and create only collateral confirmed to be missing.

The existing [Vitruveo brand library](https://www.vitruveo.ai/ecosystem/brands) contains all five original brand kits. All five public ZIP downloads were **byte-for-byte identical to the supplied ZIPs**, including all 70 SVG/PNG members. The source artwork in this repository remains the reference for logo geometry, lettering and colors. [Hash comparison](evidence/public-brandkit-comparison.json)

## Roles and naming to bring together

The Foundation describes six pillars: VTRU, Pretrend, VIBE, Scope, Protocol and Validators. These are different kinds of components, rather than six interchangeable company brands. VTRU is the network asset; Scope is an application; VIBE currently means **Vitruveo Income Building Engine**. Older cached material discussing Vibe Legal is historical context, not the current definition. [Foundation](https://www.verticalfoundation.net/)

The wider [ecosystem directory](https://www.vitruveo.ai/ecosystem) also includes HOST, VNS, bridges, Explorer and network metrics. A relationship guide should explain how these sit beneath the network and Foundation identities before expanding the logo system. The supplied artwork calls the application **VTRU Scope**, while current navigation often shortens its name to **Scope**; retain the supplied mark and confirm the editorial naming convention.

AI Wars identifies itself as a Foundation project, but is not one of the Foundation's six listed pillars. Its absence from that list does not establish discontinuation. Its main game universe, current Trends interface, and simulated current game-momentum feed need distinct descriptions. [AI Wars](https://www.epicaiwars.com/), [Trends](https://www.epicaiwars.com/trends)

## Gaps needing technical assistance

1. **Share previews:** add approved image metadata to Pretrend where missing and publish suitable Facebook/X preview metadata for Scope. Reuse existing artwork and preserve the actual source marks. [Pretrend](https://www.pretrend.ai/), [Scope](https://scope.vitruveo.ai/)
2. **Navigation:** make Scope's existing About/release-history page easier to find. Its general FAQ route rendered a heading without answers; establish whether an existing help destination should replace or complete it. [About](https://scope.vitruveo.ai/info/about), [general FAQ route](https://scope.vitruveo.ai/faqers/general)
3. **Foundation links:** standardize the intended Foundation domain. Current `.net` pages are available, while some Vitruveo links still target legacy `verticalfnd.com`. The legacy HTTPS route failed during this review; that observation does not establish a universal outage. The Foundation's VIBE CTA returned 404 at `/vibe`, while the app uses `/assets/vibe`. [Foundation](https://www.verticalfoundation.net/), [working VIBE route](https://scope.vitruveo.ai/assets/vibe)
4. **Pretrend copy:** current live protocol and whitepaper responses are V4.0 and describe VUSD. Older search snapshots showed earlier models. Some current market rulebooks still describe free/browser-local calls and future features alongside newer marketplace interfaces and an existing leaderboard. Reconcile the wording with the product owner; the presence of a UI alone does not certify operation. [Whitepaper](https://www.pretrend.ai/whitepaper/), [pump.fun market](https://www.pretrend.ai/markets/pumpfun-5/), [leaderboard](https://www.pretrend.ai/leaderboard/)
5. **AI Wars copy:** the roadmap calls Genesis/shard sale production features while the entry pages display “opening soon.” Confirm the current launch statement before publishing a campaign. Trends explicitly labels its present feed simulated until a live game feed is connected. [Roadmap](https://www.epicaiwars.com/roadmap), [Genesis](https://www.epicaiwars.com/genesis), [Mint](https://www.epicaiwars.com/mint), [Trends](https://www.epicaiwars.com/trends)

## Inventory before creating more

A separate press/contact index, approved short descriptions, partner deck and coordinated email templates are reasonable candidates. The review did not establish whether internal versions already exist. Email automation, CRM, analytics, private support systems, internal UI libraries and unpublished decks remain **unverified**, rather than missing.

Existing documentation, whitepapers, product onboarding, brand downloads and Scope About content should be linked and improved before commissioning replacements. Network metrics already exist; whether a separate incident-status/history surface exists needs its own inventory check.

## Evidence and scope

Current direct responses and rendered-app observations were preferred over stale search snippets. The findings describe public content and discoverability as observed on the research date. They are not legal ownership verification, brand-owner sign-off, an audit of contracts or funds, or a claim that every linked product function was tested.

The public repository includes concise reports, source URLs, public asset hashes and structured image metadata. Full scraped website copies, internal browser output and temporary working files are excluded.
