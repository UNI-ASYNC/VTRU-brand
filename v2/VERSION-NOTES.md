# V1 evidence and V2 revision

## Original source artwork

The five supplied ZIP files are the actual source brand-mark collections for this project. Their unchanged archives and unpacked SVG/PNG files remain in [source-brand-kits](https://github.com/UNI-ASYNC/VTRU-brand/tree/main/source-brand-kits).

The earlier research compared the five public website downloads with those uploads: the archive bytes and all 70 member hashes matched. That comparison documents source identity; it does not approve later layouts or establish legal ownership.

## V1 — preserved project evidence

V1 comprises the first [brand-system collection](https://github.com/UNI-ASYNC/VTRU-brand/tree/main/brand-system), the first [neon social collection](https://github.com/UNI-ASYNC/VTRU-brand/tree/main/social-banners), their previews and ZIPs, and the original audits and research. Those files are preserved rather than rewritten by this revision.

V1 includes new design proposals created during this work: orbital/node graphics, dark decorative panels, grids, glow backgrounds, supporting colors and proposed slogans. They were not recovered legacy brand artwork or existing website copy. The supplied logos inside those layouts came from the original kits; their source match does not make the surrounding design an established brand style.

Labels such as “original brand system” refer to the first project delivery. They do not mean that the complete layout, invented background or proposed phrase was an original official brand asset.

## V2 — separate general templates

V2 lives in the separate [v2 folder](README.md). It uses the supplied marks, neutral working canvases and existing wording recorded in the [copy-source inventory](reports/COPY-SOURCES.md). Functional bracketed fields remain available for actual announcement or document content.

The V2 rule is to copy verified website wording exactly or omit it, and to leave intended image areas open for the team's existing imagery. The revision does not invent a new visual story or promotional slogan to fill the space. White and neutral editable typography are working-template choices rather than a proposed replacement brand system.

V2's [gallery](GALLERY.md), [usage guide](USAGE-GUIDE.md) and [download location](https://github.com/UNI-ASYNC/VTRU-brand/tree/main/downloads/v2) are labelled separately. V1 remains available for comparison and evidence. The original [research report](https://github.com/UNI-ASYNC/VTRU-brand/blob/main/reports/RESEARCH-REPORT.md) and [Gaps needing technical assistance](https://github.com/UNI-ASYNC/VTRU-brand/blob/main/DEVELOPER-HANDOFF.md) handoff remain available in their existing locations.

## Review scope

The V2 website-copy check is dated **November 14, 2026**. Current wording can change later; a source URL and check date travel with each selected phrase. Reviewers can distinguish source artwork, public website wording, neutral template structure and imagery supplied later.

This revision does not claim global brand-owner approval, a new license grant for the marks, or that all earlier public-site observations have been resolved. Those are separate from preparing general templates and preserving the evidence of this work.
